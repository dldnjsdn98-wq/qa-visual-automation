## 확정 Domain / DB Relationship

Architect-selected design, pending Reviewer approval: Project, Build, Locale, Category, Situation, StringEntry, Screenshot plus supporting StringKey and SituationExpectedString. StringKey carries project-scoped string_id; StringEntry stores one Build/Locale translation. Situation-to-key mapping is many-to-many per Build, independent of Locale. Composite project foreign keys reject cross-project references, and Screenshot's composite Situation relation also enforces Category consistency. All deletes are restrictive. Text is never a key.

## Storage 정책

[Storage and failure flow](../../docs/architecture/data-flow.md): bounded PNG/JPEG uploads; original preserved; UUID-generated key; SHA-256 computed by server; filesystem adapter behind stage/publish/read/stat/delete/list operations. Exclusive maintenance reconciliation with 24-hour grace recovers orphans without racing writers.
